# Registered scope of the first follow-up

This follow-up addresses a data-reuse contract defect and describes the already
observed four-factor results. It introduces no new factors, parameter search,
candidate promotion, final-test access, broker connection or trading rule.

Cache identity must depend on the source/acquisition policy, requested symbols
and data interval rather than the whole experiment TOML. Preserve the original
experiment hash and cache manifest. A compatible legacy cache may be reused
only when its recorded source, request, interval and manifest symbols establish
the acquisition contract; missing or incompatible evidence fails clearly.

The new diagnostic registration is frozen before computing annual outputs.
It uses the existing development/validation result JSON, reports every fixed
candidate and period, groups by signal year and reports the registered flat
cost grid and positive-date concentration. It is descriptive, after aggregate
validation has been seen; it is not a fresh walk-forward test or confirmation.

Historical membership, retired-security prices and stable security identifiers
remain separate evidence gaps. A community constituent list alone does not
make today's cache point-in-time or remove survivorship bias. Do not relabel
the current universe, backfill omitted names with surviving ones, use current
sector labels as historical sectors, or purchase a source under this scope.
